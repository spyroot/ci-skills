## Port notes
- **Shared conventions.**
  - **Layout:** a thin `ci-skills/bin/<name>.py` main plus `ci-skills/lib/core/<module>.py`.
  - **Exit classes:** 0/64/65/66/69, as ci-skills already uses (binary_build.bash:35-36). The source agrees on 64 and 65 (constant.bash:355, :362; runner_config.py:56) and maps curl 22 to blocked (harbor_manager.sh:106-113).
  - **Dry-run default:** every source entry point already defaults to dry-run (toolbox_image.bash:986; toolbox_auth_smoke.bash:386; registry_sanity.bash:630; runtime_secret_sync.bash:1095; push_helm.sh:33-34).
  - **Confirm gates:**
    - Only three source entry points need a confirm before mutating: push_helm, push_images and bootstrap readiness (push_helm.sh:35-36; push_images.sh:38-39; bootstrap_readiness.bash:509-511).
    - Toolbox build, auth smoke and registry sanity mutate on a bare `--apply` (toolbox_image.bash:1007; toolbox_auth_smoke.bash:396; registry_sanity.bash:637; Makefile:178, :181).
    - The port puts every mutation behind `--apply --confirm-plan <sha256>`, where the hash covers the plan JSON. Patterns to follow: binary_build.bash:137-144, bootstrap_runner_images.bash:35-44, and bootstrap_readiness.bash:514-531 (re-checks the fingerprint just before mutating).
